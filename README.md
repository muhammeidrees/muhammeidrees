### 👋 About me

Some ideas begin with a question. Others begin with a problem. I like finding out where they can lead.

**Before I build, I want to understand.** I'm curious about why things work, where they fall short, and what might work better. I read, question, study the mathematics, explore existing research, and experiment with my own ideas. Sometimes the answer is already out there. Sometimes there's something worth solving.

> I don't believe in reinventing everything. I believe in understanding what exists and knowing when it needs to change.

<p align="center">
  <code>Question</code> → <code>Understand</code> → <code>Explore</code> → <code>Experiment</code> → <code>Build</code> → <code>Improve</code>
</p>
